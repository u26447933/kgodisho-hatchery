About Us

Kgodisho Hatchery is a trusted poultry producer located in Cloetesdam Village, Ga-Matlala, Polokwane, Limpopo. We specialize in breeding and supplying healthy broiler chickens, day-old chicks, dressed chickens, table eggs and poultry products for families, farmers, stockvels and businesses across Limpopo Province.
